1. Executive Summary

Supplier Resilience is an enterprise-grade supply chain risk management solution built on SAP BTP. It uses a multi-agent architecture to detect, assess, and respond to supply chain disruptions.

Key Capabilities

CapabilityDescription
Disruption DetectionCapture PO delays, quality issues, and supplier risks
Risk Assessment100-point scoring algorithm with 5 weighted components
Inventory Survival AnalysisCalculate how long inventory lasts vs supplier recovery
Live S/4HANA IntegrationFetch real-time PO data from S/4HANA
Control Tower DashboardCentralized view of all supply chain risks
